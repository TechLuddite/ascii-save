# Artwork notice

The prints are by Katsushika Hokusai (1760–1849) and Utagawa Hiroshige (1797–1858). The Art Institute of Chicago identifies each of these 32 works as public domain and supplies its images under [Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images). Each record's `is_public_domain` flag was checked when its image was retrieved on 2026-10-02.

These colour block conversions derive from those public-domain images. They are not newly authored artworks and are not relicensed as original MIT artwork. The repository's MIT license covers its code. Museum credit lines and source identifiers are preserved in `SOURCE-CREDITS.md` and `manifest.json`. The museum does not endorse this project.

The museum's descriptive texts, which it licenses separately under CC BY, are not reproduced. Original JPEG downloads and local preview captures are excluded from Git.
